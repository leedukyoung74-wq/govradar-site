How the service works

From request to delivery: what you get, by when, and to what standard — promised up front.

Step by step

  1. 1
    Jelajahi (gratis)

    Lihat program pemerintah, tender, dan risiko 193 negara tanpa mendaftar. Simpan negara untuk menjadi anggota gratis.

  2. 2
    Permintaan

    Pick a plan or product on the pricing page and send the form. The more you tell us about your sector, countries and needs, the sharper the result.

  3. 3
    Quote (1 business day)

    Within one business day we email a quote and schedule. Companies get an e-tax invoice; public bodies also get business registration and bank details.

  4. 4
    Payment & start (1 business day)

    Within one business day of payment we activate your access and send a welcome email. Custom work starts with a 5-question kick-off form.

  5. 5
    Receive

    Anggota menerima ringkasan email negara pilihan setiap Senin (saat ini dikirim setelah ditinjau staf). Ringkasan tiba dalam 5 hari kerja, tinjauan kesesuaian dalam 3 hari.

  6. 6
    Revisions & questions

    Satu revisi gratis dalam 7 hari setelah laporan khusus diserahkan. Pertanyaan email dijawab dalam satu hari kerja.

  7. 7
    Check-in

    Pada hari ke-30 kami mengirim survei 3 pertanyaan dan saran langkah berikutnya (tender, mitra, program dukungan). Klien institusi mendapat tinjauan daring triwulanan.
