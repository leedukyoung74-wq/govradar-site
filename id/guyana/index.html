Guyana's 2026 budget of GY$1.558 trillion (about US$7.5bn) is 307% larger than in 2021, with large allocations to roads and bridges, education, health, housing and energy. Oil revenue is funding the Gas-to-Energy project and non-oil growth.

Agenda nasional

  • Oil-funded (Natural Resource Fund) infrastructure and social investment
  • Gas-to-Energy project aiming to roughly halve electricity costs
  • Roads and bridges (GY$196.1bn) and housing
  • Cost-of-living relief via cash transfers and a higher income-tax threshold
  • Non-oil growth (agriculture, manufacturing, tourism) and 'smart country' digitisation
