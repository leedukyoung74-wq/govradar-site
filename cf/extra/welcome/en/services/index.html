01 · COUNTRY

Country data

Key agendas, leaders, budgets, programme folders, credit ratings and sanctions for 193 governments as country reports. Changes are kept as history and corrections.

VIEW

02 · TENDER

Tenders & projects

Only open tenders from national e-procurement and World Bank, ADB, AfDB, IDB and UNGM notices. We show eligibility, local-partner needs and consortium options, and alert you 7 and 3 days before closing.

03 · SITUATION

World situation

War and conflict, natural disasters such as earthquakes and typhoons, and epidemics collected daily from USGS, GDACS, WHO and NOAA, with country risk levels on a map.

04 · READY

Preparedness

Work out how much water, food and essentials to stock for your household and period, and make an evacuation plan and emergency contact card. We also present the WMM GO72, HOME3 and CAR24 kits.

05 · CONNECT

CONNECT

We connect government programmes with companies, and companies with each other: shortlist, introduction and first meeting, and a 90-day sprint. Introductions only with both sides’ consent. No contract or award is guaranteed.
