Kenya's KSh 4.82 trillion FY2026/27 budget follows the Bottom-Up Economic Transformation Agenda, prioritising agriculture, affordable housing, health, education and small businesses. The government has set up a National Infrastructure Fund and a Sovereign Wealth Fund and will announce a successor to Vision 2030 after public consultation.
