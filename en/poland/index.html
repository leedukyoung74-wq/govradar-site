Poland

Updated 2026-10-29 · as of 2027 예산안(2026-08 내각 승인)·2026-10 정부 발표 · Stable

Poland is running defence spending above 4% of GDP alongside mega-projects such as the new hub airport, high-speed rail and its first nuclear plant, while the 2027 draft budget deficit exceeds 7% of GDP. Frequent presidential vetoes often delay government legislation.

Government · leader

President Karol Nawrocki / Prime Minister Donald Tusk

Key national agenda

  • Defence build-up (about 4.5% of GDP in 2027) and local arms production
  • Higher health spending
  • Energy security and transition, including the first nuclear plant
  • Major infrastructure (Port Polska hub airport, high-speed rail, ports)
  • Deficit control under the EU excessive deficit procedure
  • Trade and investment diversification toward Asia
