Key national agenda

  • Stronger security (extortion and organized crime; police extortion unit and AI video surveillance)
  • El Niño and disaster prevention, river cleanup
  • Human development: child anemia, maternal health, vaccination
  • Higher productivity and better public investment (incl. more mining permits)
  • Rule of law and an investment-friendly framework
