Government · leader

Prime Minister Philip Davis (PLP, re-elected May 2026) / Head of State King Charles III

Key national agenda

  • Cost-of-living relief: VAT removed on unprepared food (from 1 Apr 2026), lower power bills
  • Energy transition: 70MW shore power at Arawak Cay, 60MW solar + 10MWh battery PPAs
  • Health infrastructure: new New Providence hospital, Family Island clinics
  • Affordable housing and skills (Upskill Bahamas, 25,000 places by 2031) (single source)
  • Digital government: Sand Dollar in government payments, AI Governance Act
  • Grand Bahama revival: power company acquisition and future-framework consultation
