How the service works

From request to delivery: what you get, by when, and to what standard — promised up front.

Step by step

  1. 1
    Explore (free)

    Browse government programmes, tenders and risk for 193 countries without signing up. Save countries to become a free member.

  2. 2
    Request

    Pick a plan or product on the pricing page and send the form. The more you tell us about your sector, countries and needs, the sharper the result.

  3. 3
    Quote (1 business day)

    Within one business day we email a quote and schedule. Companies get an e-tax invoice; public bodies also get business registration and bank details.

  4. 4
    Payment & start (1 business day)

    Within one business day of payment we activate your access and send a welcome email. Custom work starts with a 5-question kick-off form.

  5. 5
    Receive

    Members get a Monday email summary for their countries (currently sent after staff review). Briefs arrive within 5 business days, tender fit reviews within 3.

  6. 6
    Revisions & questions

    One free revision within 7 days of delivery for custom reports. Email questions are answered within one business day.

  7. 7
    Check-in

    On day 30 we send a 3-question survey and next-step suggestions (tenders, partners, support schemes). Institutional clients get a quarterly online review.

FAQ

How do I pay?

For now, by bank transfer after you receive a quote. Card payment is coming.

Can I change my countries?

Yes, email us any time and the next weekly summary will reflect it.

Can several people use it?

An analysis membership is for one person. For teams, choose an institutional licence.

Why is the data free?

Public facts like programmes and tenders can be found by anyone, even with AI. We charge for what comes next: analysis, alerts, introductions and execution.

What if something is wrong?

Email 73036396@naver.com. We check and correct within two business days and log the correction.

Do you submit bids for us?

You decide and submit. We help with information, fit reviews, draft proposals and partner introductions.

Send feedback

Anyone can send it. We review feedback weekly and act on it.
