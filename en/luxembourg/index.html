The 2027 draft budget cuts the general government deficit to 0.6% of GDP (EUR 570m) and focuses on fighting poverty, middle-class purchasing power and tax relief. Under the 8 Jun 2026 tripartite deal (Resilienzpak), the government is extending energy support and rolling out a housing supply roadmap.

Key national agenda

  • 2027 budget: fighting poverty, a stronger middle class, purchasing power and tax relief, sound finances
  • Implementing the Resilienzpak tripartite deal and extending energy measures
  • Five-axis affordable housing plan (unused land, faster building, investment, household support, landlord-tenant balance)
  • Keeping public debt below 30% of GDP (28.6% projected for 2030)
  • Deeper EU and regional cooperation (Benelux and European Council)
