Key national agenda

  • Raise defence spending to 3.5% of GDP by 2030
  • Welfare: schools and daycare, free dental care for vulnerable groups (all citizens by 2035), higher pensions for the lowest-income pensioners
  • Green transition: electrification, phasing out fossil fuels and energy self-sufficiency
  • Phase in free public transport for under-22s
  • Middle-bracket tax relief to reward extra work
  • Investment and closer ties with Greenland
