GOV RADAR / Saudi Arabia

How to sell to the Government of Saudi Arabia

Checked 2026-10-31

A Saudi Commercial Registration (CR) is required to access Etimad. Early 2026 changes to the regional-headquarters (RHQ) rule let non-RHQ foreign firms win only in two defined cases.

Procurement portals

  • Etimad
  • Monshaat (중소기업청)

How to take part

  1. Prepare CR, MISA investment licence, Chamber membership, Zakat certificate, corporate bank account, VAT and GOSI registration
  2. Sector approvals: construction (Bina), training (TVTC), insurance (SAMA)
  3. Open an Etimad account and check the method: general, limited (usually up to five firms), two-stage (RFI→RFP) or framework
  4. Consulting is often 70–80% technical score; construction and supply weigh price more
  5. Build an Arabic document library early

Local preference & limits

  • Non-RHQ firms win only if (1) at most one technically compliant bid exists, or (2) they rank top technically and are at least 25% cheaper than the second-best
  • Contracts up to SAR 1 million (about US$266,000) are exempt from the RHQ rule
  • Check local content (LCGPA) requirements separately

Bid language · Mostly Arabic (portal not fully in English)

Good to know

  • Monshaat-certified SMEs get extra points and bid-bond exemption
  • Many buyers score technical and financial proposals separately
