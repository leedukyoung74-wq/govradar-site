Gouvernement · dirigeant

President Patrick Herminie (United Seychelles; won Oct 2025 run-off; head of state and government)

Priorités nationales

  • Affordable housing: 200+ houses and 120+ apartments in 2026
  • Land reclamation phase 1: 570,000+ m2 and three new islands
  • Digital government: 75% of services online by 2028
  • Renewable Energy Acceleration Programme and rooftop solar
  • Cut public debt to 50% of GDP by 2030
  • National e-procurement system (tendering to launch in 2026)
