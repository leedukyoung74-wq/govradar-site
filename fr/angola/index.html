The Council of Ministers chaired by President Lourenço approved an AOA 37.34 trillion 2027 budget on 27 October 2026; parliament holds the general vote on 12 November. Based on $71 oil and about 5.5% growth, it prioritises roads, energy, water and education, with tight fiscal room ahead of the 2027 elections.
