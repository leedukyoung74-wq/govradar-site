Saudi Arabia

Saudi Arabia · SA · updated 2026-11-14

Summary Key agenda Government programmes Tenders Promising business

Summary

On 1 October the Ministry of Finance issued its pre-budget statement for 2027: spending of about SAR 1,392 billion and revenue of about SAR 1,202 billion, a deficit of 3.6% of GDP. The government plans to keep spending on non-oil diversification and reprioritised giga-projects.

Key agenda

Vision 2030 diversification and a larger private sector

Fiscal sustainability (deficit 3.6% of GDP)

Priority to fast-return sectors such as AI and data centres, tourism, logistics and mining

Preparing Expo 2030 Riyadh and the 2034 FIFA World Cup

Localisation (local content) and procurement reform
