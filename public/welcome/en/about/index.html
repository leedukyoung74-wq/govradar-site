Data principles

Two or more sources

Key facts such as leaders, budgets and deadlines are stated plainly only when two independent sources agree.

Official sources first

We start with governments, international organisations and procurement portals, then cross-check with wire services and major media.

Labelled and public

A single source is marked ‘single source’; statements by a party to a conflict are marked ‘party claim’.

Correction log

When we fix a published fact, we record what changed and when.
