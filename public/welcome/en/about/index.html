Data principles

Two or more sources

Key facts such as leaders, budgets and deadlines are stated plainly only when two independent sources agree.

Official sources first

We start with governments, international organisations and procurement portals, then cross-check with wire services and major media.

Labelled and public

A single source is marked ‘single source’; statements by a party to a conflict are marked ‘party claim’.

Correction log

When we fix a published fact, we record what changed and when.

HISTORY

GOV RADAR history

  1. 2026.11

    Global government programme network expanded

    Country-by-country research on government policy, budgeted programmes and overseas business opportunities was extended to all 193 UN member states.

  2. 2026.11

    International tender and programme search launched

    A tender and deadline board went live for browsing national government programmes and international procurement notices by deadline, sector, contracting authority and original source.

  3. 2026.11

    World risk information expanded

    The world situation board and related pages were extended so that war and conflict, natural disasters, epidemics, cyber attacks and other international risks can be checked by country.

  4. 2026.11

    Preparedness centre built

    Step-by-step guides and calculators for 30 days, 60 days, 90 days and 1 year help individuals, households and organisations prepare drinking water, food, backup power, communications and evacuation plans.

  5. 2026.11

    Information reliability principles set

    Four data principles were published: official sources such as governments and international organisations first, cross-checking of key facts, labelling uncertainty, and keeping a correction record.

  6. 2025.01

    GOV RADAR project started

    Work began on a global radar that links the policy direction and public programmes of governments worldwide with Korean companies’ overseas expansion and international business opportunities.

  7. 2021.12

    Operating company WMM founded

    WORLD MAKING MEMBERS Co., Ltd. is presented as the operating base of GOV RADAR. The founding month will be confirmed after a final check of the corporate register.
