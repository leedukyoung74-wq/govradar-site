Three bodies lead the projects

A Korean company, a UK-headquartered network and a consortium formed for each project share the work of planning and delivery.

Project tiers

Four tiers, by total project cost and how the work is shared.

Gigaproject

Total cost of US$10 billion or more

Work such as national infrastructure, new cities and energy, where several countries and institutions move together for more than a decade.

Megaproject

Total cost of US$1 billion or more

Large projects such as industrial parks, power plants and transport networks, with governments and international finance taking part.

Strategic consortium project

Delivered by a consortium, whatever the size

Several organisations split the roles and bid, propose and deliver together.

Project

Delivered by the headquarters

Planned and run directly by World Making Members or GCSME.

The giga and mega thresholds follow Oxford professor Bent Flyvbjerg's classification of large projects (US$1bn+ mega, US$10bn+ giga).
