How to sell to the Government of Indonesia

تم التحقق في 2026-10-31

Not always legally required, but a local agent, distributor or representative is effectively needed. Selling on e-Catalogue requires a locally registered Indonesian entity.

بوابات المشتريات

  • LPSE / SPSE (e-bidding)
  • LKPP (national procurement agency)

كيفية المشاركة

  1. Track the buyer's LPSE (ministries use LKPP's central system; regions are adopting it)
  2. Set up a local entity or partner (mandatory for e-Catalogue)
  3. Register medicines, devices and food with BPOM or the Ministry of Health first
  4. Know the method: e-purchasing, direct purchase, direct appointment, limited or open tender
  5. Plan local content (TKDN) where it applies

أفضلية المحلي والقيود

  • TKDN minimum local content in certain sectors and contracts
  • Foreign participation limited in some strategic projects

لغة العطاء · Bahasa Indonesia

  • Infrastructure need about US$437bn under the 2020–2024 RPJMN (energy, transport, digital, water)
