How to sell to the Government of Brazil

تم التحقق في 2026-10-31

Since October 2020 foreign companies can bid without first setting up a local entity or representative; a local partner is needed at contract signing. SICAF registration still asks for a legal representative in Brazil with power to receive service of process. Law 14.133/2021 also lets foreign firms lead consortia.

بوابات المشتريات

  • PNCP (Portal Nacional de Contratações Públicas)
  • Compras.gov.br (federal procurement portal, incl. SICAF)

كيفية المشاركة

  1. Find notices on PNCP; the bidding platform is named in each notice (federal tenders mostly run on Compras.gov.br).
  2. Create a SICAF user account on Compras.gov.br; foreign firms register with a home-country identifier such as TIN or DUNS.
  3. Upload documents for Level I (company data, Brazilian legal representative), II (legal standing), III–IV (no tax/labour debts), V (technical qualification), VI (financial statements, no bankruptcy).
  4. Free translations are accepted at first; before signing, replace them with sworn-translator translations and apostilled or consularised documents.
  5. Price the bid according to the tax treatment set in the tender notice (edital).

أفضلية المحلي والقيود

  • Preference margin of up to 10% for national goods and services meeting Brazilian technical standards (Decree 11.890/2024, federal bodies).
  • Up to 10% more for goods/services from R&D and innovation done in Brazil; total capped at 20%.
  • Bidders cannot be distinguished by domestic or foreign ownership except where a preference applies.
  • Architecture and engineering projects must use BIM or equivalent international standards.

لغة العطاء · Portuguese. Foreign documents: free translation first, sworn translation in Brazil before contract.

مشاريع بنوك التنمية

Eligible foreign firms can bid on MDB-financed procurement (World Bank, IDB, etc.). The US Commercial Guide states that a legal entity in Brazil is required for MDB-financed procurement; check each bank's rules.

معلومات مفيدة

  • Price, payment terms and financing are often decisive.
  • Be ready to present financing, engineering, equipment, training and after-sales service.
  • Learn sector-specific regulations before bidding.
  • Start sworn translations and apostilles early.
  • Key sectors: infrastructure (roads, rail, airports, housing, telecom), health, security, defence.
