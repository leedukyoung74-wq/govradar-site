Member analysis requests

We start with four requests whose output is fixed in advance. Before you send, you see what you will receive, how it counts against your limit, and how it is handled.

For analysis members (KRW 19,800 a month): two requests a month are included. Non-members and extra requests get a quote by email first, and we start only after you agree.

1. Choose a request

Handling · We confirm receipt by email within one to two business days. Every result states the conclusion, the evidence, what could not be confirmed and the next step. If we cannot carry out a request, we tell you why.

Limit: two requests a month for analysis members; unused requests do not carry over. Turnaround depends on the request and the material, and our confirmation email gives the expected time first (usually 7–10 business days). Follow-up questions or a wider scope count as a new request. Results are a reference review based on public information and do not guarantee legal, investment or tender outcomes. The per-tender fit assessment (KRW 100,000) on the pricing page is the detailed version with document list and consortium plan; member requests are a quick check of eligibility and gaps.
