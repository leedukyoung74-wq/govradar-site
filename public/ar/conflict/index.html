Saudi Arabia (Iran war, Houthi attacks) حرب

Target of Iranian and Houthi missiles and drones · leads coalition air strikes in Yemen

  • مؤكَّدAfter the Iran war began on 28 Feb 2026, Iranian missiles and drones hit Saudi refineries; Aramco shut the Ras Tanura refinery.[35][36]
  • مؤكَّدRight after the April US–Iran ceasefire, an attack on an East–West pipeline pumping station cut flows by 700,000 bpd (Saudi official). Iran denied involvement.[37][38]
  • مؤكَّدIn May Reuters and AP, citing US officials, reported that Saudi Arabia and the UAE secretly struck drone and missile launch sites inside Iran.[39][40]
  • مؤكَّدIn early October the Houthis hit southern airports such as Abha and Jizan with missiles and drones, wounding three (Saudi statement); on 8 Oct the coalition said it intercepted three ballistic missiles.[41][42][43]
  • بيان رسميThe Saudi-led coalition said 100 jets gave air support to the Yemeni government's 'Yemen Dawn' offensive launched on 4 Oct and destroyed 324 Houthi targets.[44]

Re-plan safety for sites and trips in the Eastern Province (refining) and the south (Jizan, Abha, Najran); review Red Sea and Gulf shipping insurance and delivery dates.
