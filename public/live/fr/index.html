Prepare now — what to do today

For each risk on the radar, what a household or company can do today. Based on official guidance (Korea's national disaster safety portal, US Ready.gov and others).

War and armed conflict

  1. Find your nearest public shelter in advance.
  2. Agree how your family will reach each other and where to meet if separated.
  3. Keep ID and passport copies, some cash and basic medicines in one bag.

Blackouts and cyberattacks

  1. Keep a torch, batteries, a power bank and a portable radio.
  2. Back up important files somewhere disconnected from the internet.
  3. Change passwords on work and bank accounts and turn on two-step login.

Earthquake, tsunami, volcano

  1. Fix heavy furniture to walls; keep shoes and a torch by the bed.
  2. When shaking stops, leave by the stairs, not the lift.
  3. On the coast, if you feel strong shaking, go to high ground without waiting for a warning.

Typhoon, flood, wildfire

  1. Turn on emergency alerts and weather warnings.
  2. Avoid basements, riverbanks and low ground; clear drains in advance.
  3. In a wildfire, evacuate upwind towards wide roads.

Epidemics

  1. Wash hands, wear a mask and stay home when ill.
  2. Keep basic medicines and a thermometer; follow public-health notices.
  3. Check in advance that remote work is possible.

Economic crisis, prices, food

  1. Keep 1–2 weeks of long-life food, eating and replacing it as you go.
  2. Separate essential spending and an emergency fund.
  3. Firms reliant on raw materials or shipping should line up at least one alternative supplier.

✓ Basic 72 hours: about 4 litres of water per person per day (US Ready.gov) for at least 3 days, 3 days of food that needs no cooking, torch, radio, power bank, first-aid kit, cash and ID copies.
