Government · leader

President Alexander Stubb / Prime Minister Petteri Orpo (National Coalition-led coalition)

Key national agenda

  • Fiscal consolidation and compliance with the EU excessive-deficit spending path
  • Corporate tax cut from 20% to 18% and income tax relief for low and middle earners
  • Higher defence spending and continued support for Ukraine
  • Employment measures such as youth and long-term unemployed vouchers
  • Housing renovation and construction stimulus
  • Road, rail and port infrastructure investment
