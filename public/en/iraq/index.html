Government · leader

President Nizar Amidi (elected April 2026) / Prime Minister Ali Faleh al-Zaidi (took office May 2026)

Key national agenda

  • Expanding power generation (target to double output in 2027, including 12,000 MW of solar)
  • Diversifying oil export routes (Ceyhan, Baniyas, Aqaba) and raising output
  • Fiscal reform: IQD 17.8tn of savings within a year, more non-oil revenue
  • Government digitisation, including ASYCUDA customs automation
  • Security reform: state monopoly on arms and a timeline for foreign troop withdrawal
  • Distributing one million residential plots and rebuilding agriculture
