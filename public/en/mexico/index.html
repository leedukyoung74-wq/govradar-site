On 15 October the Chamber of Deputies passed the 2027 Revenue Law, with MXN 10.6 trillion in revenue and MXN 1.7 trillion in new borrowing; the spending budget (PEF) is due to be finalised in November. With growth forecasts cut, the government is raising collection without new taxes and using Plan México to attract industrial-park and nearshoring investment.
