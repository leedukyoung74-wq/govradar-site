Key national agenda

  • Tech self-reliance and 'new quality productive forces' (chips, biotech, quantum)
  • AI+ upgrades for commerce, consumption and care services
  • Removing service-sector entry barriers and expanding private investment
  • Demographic response: birth-friendly society, more vocational training
  • Binding green targets: 17% lower carbon intensity, 25% non-fossil energy by 2030
