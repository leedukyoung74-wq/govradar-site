리비아 · LY

Updated 2026-11-14 · as of 2026-04-12 · High

In April 2026 the eastern House of Representatives and the western High Council of State agreed Libya's first unified budget in 13 years, LYD 190bn (about $30bn). It allots LYD 40bn to development and LYD 12bn to the National Oil Corporation, with development priorities set by a joint committee under central bank supervision. Haftar's forces control major oil fields and export terminals.
