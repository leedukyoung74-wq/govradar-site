Key national agenda

  • Fiscal consolidation: 5% of GDP primary surplus, 4.9% deficit, debt at 78% of GDP by June 2027
  • Implementing the IMF reform programme and cutting external debt
  • More human-development spending (education +25%, health +39.5%)
  • Attracting manufacturing and logistics investment to the Suez Canal Economic Zone
  • Export promotion (EGP 90bn in the FY2026/27 budget)
  • Expanding energy output and local development projects
