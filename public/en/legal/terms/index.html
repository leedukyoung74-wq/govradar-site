Terms of Service

1. Purpose

These terms govern use of GOV RADAR (radar.wmm-hoa.com, the 'Service') operated by WORLD MAKING MEMBERS Co., Ltd. (the 'Company').

2. What the Service is

The Service collects and summarises public information on government policy, projects, tenders, multilateral procurement and risk, and offers members alerts, AI analysis, consortium matching and online document support. It is an information service, not investment, legal, tax or security advice.

3. Accounts

Anyone 14 or older may register by email. Under-14s need a parent's consent and apply separately. False or third-party details may lead to suspension.

4. Fees

There is a free tier (FREE) and paid services (analysis membership, market-entry briefs, tender fit reviews, project partnerships, institutional and university licences), priced on the Orders & pricing page. Paid services are currently prepaid by bank transfer against a quote and do not renew automatically. When online subscriptions open, the checkout will show the amount, billing cycle, next charge date and how to cancel, and ask for separate consent. Price changes are notified 30 days ahead.

5. Refunds

See the separate Refund Policy, which follows Korean consumer-protection law for e-commerce.

6. Accuracy

We cross-check public sources and show a verdict and sources for each item, but source changes, errors and AI limitations mean some content may differ from reality. Always check the original notice or government statement before bidding, contracting, investing or travelling.

7. Scenarios

'Preparedness scenarios' are hypotheses for planning; they are neither predictions nor verified facts.

Paid work is open to people aged 19+ (or the age of majority where they live, if higher) who pass a skills task and identity check before payout, under deliverable-based service contracts. No working hours are set; fees are fixed per deliverable and paid after acceptance.
