Brunei

브루나이 · BN · Commonwealth

Updated 2026-10-30 · as of 2026-10 · Stable

Brunei is diversifying away from oil and gas under Wawasan Brunei 2035, with the non-oil sector now 54% of GDP and 60% of exports. On 2026-10-26 the economy minister pledged to turn 27 investment projects worth about USD 6 billion into jobs and exports.

Government · leader

Key national agenda

  • Economic diversification under Wawasan Brunei 2035
  • Downstream petrochemicals (Hengyi phase 2) and advanced manufacturing
  • Agri-food, fisheries and food security (Brunei-Singapore Agri-Tech Food Zone)
  • Telecoms, digital and creative industries; transport and logistics
  • 30% renewable share of installed capacity by 2035
  • Skills training matched to employer needs
