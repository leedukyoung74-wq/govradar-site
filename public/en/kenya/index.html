Key national agenda

  • Bottom-Up Economic Transformation Agenda: agriculture, affordable housing, health, MSMEs
  • Infrastructure financing via the National Infrastructure Fund and Sovereign Wealth Fund
  • Universal health coverage and a stronger Social Health Authority
  • Youth jobs (NextGen.ke) and wider higher-education funding
  • A new long-term national vision after Vision 2030
