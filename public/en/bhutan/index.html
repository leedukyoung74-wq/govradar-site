Bhutan

부탄 · BT

Updated 2026-11-14 · as of 2026-27 · Stable

PM Tobgay's 2026-27 budget is Nu 153.3bn (about 30% of the 13th Plan outlay), with capital spending of Nu 72.5bn (+16%). Under 'Building Resilience and Self-Reliance' it raises education and health (super-specialty and mother-and-child hospitals in Mongar) and builds Gelephu Mindfulness City as an investment hub. Growth is forecast at 6.3% in 2026 and 7.6% in 2027 (hydropower).
