Government · leader

Key national agenda

  • Economic recovery: prioritise local investors while courting foreign investment
  • Expand trade and investment ties with ASEAN and neighbours (visits to Malaysia, Thailand, India)
  • Address fuel and power shortages and attract solar and other energy investment
  • Grow export manufacturing (footwear, tyres, leather, rubber)
  • Maintain security and stability (defence and security spending prioritised)
