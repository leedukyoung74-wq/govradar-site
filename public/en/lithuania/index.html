Lithuania

LT

Updated 2026-11-07 · as of 2026-10 · Stable

After PM Ruginienė resigned, Social Democrat leader Sinkevičius formed a coalition government in July 2026. The 2027 draft budget keeps defence at about EUR 4.8bn (about 5% of GDP) while raising pensions, widening the deficit to 3.8% of GDP.
