North Macedonia

북마케도니아 · MK

Updated 2026-11-14 · as of 2026-10-30 · Stable

The Mickoski government's 2027 draft budget spends MKD 440.4bn (about €7.2bn, +4.8%) with a deficit of 3.5% of GDP. It allots MKD 40.8bn to capital investment, MKD 220.8bn to pensions and social transfers and 2% of GDP to defence; ongoing infrastructure projects are estimated at about €10bn.
