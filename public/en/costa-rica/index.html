Government · leader

President Laura Fernández Delgado (head of state and government, took office 2026-05-08, term 2026-2030)

Key national agenda

  • Organised-crime crackdown and security (new mega-prison, security cooperation with the US)
  • Five constitutional reforms (limits on Assembly approval of foreign loans and a debt cap, shorter Comptroller term, etc.)
  • Bill for a 15% tax on foreign interest, dividends, rent and capital gains
  • Bilateral-FTA trade strategy after shelving CPTPP (response to the 12.5% US tariff)
  • Road infrastructure such as widening Route 1 (San José-San Ramón)

Government programmes

2027 national budget¢12.4 trillion

Passed first debate with 31 ruling-party (PPSO) votes. It cuts or freezes deferred university funds and Judiciary allocations. The deadline for second debate is 29 November.
