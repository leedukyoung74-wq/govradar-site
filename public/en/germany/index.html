Germany

독일 · DE

Updated 2026-11-15 · as of 2027 budget in parliament (2026-11) · Stable

The CDU/CSU-SPD coalition's 2027 budget (about EUR 555.4bn) centres on defence, infrastructure and state modernisation. The Bundestag budget committee holds its all-night clean-up session in the third week of November, with final deliberation planned for the 23-27 November sitting week. Rising AfD support and the pension dispute weigh on the coalition.

Government · leader

Federal President Frank-Walter Steinmeier (Head of State); Chancellor Friedrich Merz, CDU (Head of Government)

Key national agenda

  • Building up defence (core 2027 defence budget about EUR 109.7bn)
  • Repairing bridges, roads and rail through the infrastructure and climate-neutrality special fund
  • State modernisation: digital administration, less red tape, implementing the EU AI Act
  • Restoring competitiveness with cheaper industrial power and grid-fee subsidies (2027-2029)
  • Stabilising pension and health-insurance finances (including a sugary-drinks tax)
