Government · leader

Key national agenda

  • Building up defence (core 2027 defence budget about EUR 109.7bn)
  • Repairing bridges, roads and rail through the infrastructure and climate-neutrality special fund
  • State modernisation: digital administration, less red tape, implementing the EU AI Act
  • Restoring competitiveness with cheaper industrial power and grid-fee subsidies (2027-2029)
  • Stabilising pension and health-insurance finances (including a sugary-drinks tax)
