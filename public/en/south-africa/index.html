South Africa

남아프리카공화국 · ZA · Commonwealth

Updated 2026-10-29 · as of 2026-10-21 · Stable

In its 21 Oct 2026 Medium-Term Budget Policy Statement, the Treasury cut the 2026 growth forecast to 1.2% and set a path to shrink the deficit from 4.3% of GDP to 2.5% by 2030. Infrastructure finance (IFISA, BFI, PPPs), water and power reform, and stronger municipal finances are central, with local elections due on 4 Nov 2026.

  • Tackling gender-based violence and femicide while sustaining social grants
