Our commitments to individuals

  • No sign-up, training or deposit fees, ever
  • Work becomes available only when clients place orders; we never promise income for signing up
  • Paid work is open only to people aged 19 or over (or the local age of majority, if higher)
  • We never ask for ID photos; identity is verified only before the first payment
  • Same work, same pay, regardless of nationality

Rates are indicative. Tax: Korean residents 3.3% (business income) or 8.8% (other income) withheld; for residents abroad, tax is handled under the rules of their country of residence.
