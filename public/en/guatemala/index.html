Key national agenda

  • Anti-corruption and stronger democratic institutions
  • More infrastructure spending (13% of the 2027 budget, Q24.9bn)
  • Airport and aviation safety upgrades (US$250m World Bank loan)
  • Transmission grid expansion (PET-4 tender in preparation) and a one-stop permitting bill
  • Public security and managing the 2027 election
