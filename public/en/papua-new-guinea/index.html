Government · leader

Head of state King Charles III (Governor-General Grand Chief Sir Bob Dadae); Prime Minister James Marape

Key national agenda

  • Fiscal consolidation and a balanced 2027 budget (deficit near 1%)
  • Papua LNG (US$14.5 billion) FID and securing resource revenue
  • Free and fair 2027 national election (K200 million for the Electoral Commission)
  • El Niño and drought response, fuel price relief
  • Public service modernisation including AI
  • Constitutional process on Bougainville's political future
