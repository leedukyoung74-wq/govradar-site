Key national agenda

Korean support schemes

  • Export Voucher Program · Ministry of SMEs and Startups / Ministry of Trade (Export Voucher) — SMEs and mid-sized firms buy overseas marketing and consulting services with vouchers; in 2027 it splits into a foundation voucher and a growth package.
  • KOTRA overseas market entry support · KOTRA — Provides market research, buyer matching and trade show support through overseas trade offices.
  • KOSME export and overseas expansion policy loans · Korea SMEs and Startups Agency (KOSME) — Runs policy funds for exporting SMEs' new-market entry and stability, increased by the 2026 supplementary budget.
