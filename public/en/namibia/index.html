Namibia

나미비아 · NA · Commonwealth

Updated 2026-10-30 · as of 2026/27 중기예산 검토 (2026-10-22) · Stable

In its Oct 22, 2026 mid-year review Namibia cut its revenue outlook on weak mining and raised the budget by about N$700 million (N$87.9bn to N$88.6bn), with public debt projected at N$201.7 billion. Final investment decisions on green hydrogen (Hyphen) and the offshore Venus oil field are delayed, while land and petroleum bills are contested.

Government · leader

President Netumbo Nandi-Ndaitwah (since Mar 2025) / Vice President Lucia Witbooi / Prime Minister Elijah Ngurare

Key national agenda

  • Fiscal health: medium-term revenue and debt management strategies (before Feb 2027)
  • Green hydrogen (Hyphen, 24% state stake) and offshore oil (Venus)
  • Consolidated land law and a ban on foreign land acquisition
  • Moving the upstream petroleum unit to the Presidency (petroleum amendment bill)
  • Farm support including foot-and-mouth control and drought preparedness
  • Leaner government: ministries cut from 21 to 14
