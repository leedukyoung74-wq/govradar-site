Government · leader

President Hakainde Hichilema (re-elected Aug 2026, second term from Sep 2026)

Key national agenda

  • Grow Zambia: jobs, single-digit inflation and exchange rate stability
  • Copper output from about 890,000 tonnes (2025) to 3 million tonnes by 2031, with more local processing
  • More power supply (at least 2,000 MW extra needed for copper growth) and better logistics
  • Broader tax base and digital tax systems
  • Seek a new IMF programme within 2026
  • Regional transport corridors such as Lobito
