Government · leader

President Luis Abinader (head of state and government, second term ending 2028-08-16)

Key national agenda

  • Investment grade by 2028 and fiscal consolidation
  • Long-term goals for 2036: double GDP, create 1.7 million jobs, end extreme poverty
  • More transport infrastructure investment (roads, metro)
  • Cutting electricity distribution losses and the subsidy burden
  • Migration enforcement and counter-narcotics cooperation with the US
