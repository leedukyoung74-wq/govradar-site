Botswana

보츠와나 · BW · Commonwealth

Updated 2026-10-30 · as of 2026-10 (2026/27 예산 기준) · Stable

After contractions in 2024 and 2025 driven by weak diamond demand, Botswana targets 3.1% growth in 2026 and is shifting from state-led to private, export-led growth under an 'economic transformation and fiscal prudence' agenda. An October 2026 state visit to China upgraded ties to a comprehensive strategic cooperative partnership.

Government · leader

President Duma Gideon Boko (head of state and government); Vice President and Finance Minister Ndaba Gaolathe

Key national agenda

  • Diversification away from diamonds (NDP12 and the BETP transformation programme)
  • Rebuilding fiscal buffers and revenue (e-invoicing, VAT on remote services, proposed top-bracket and corporate tax rises)
  • Digital transformation, including a national e-procurement system
  • Building manufacturing, moving beyond 'tenderpreneurship', procurement preference for local manufacturers
  • Integrated energy, water and transport infrastructure
  • Health reform (Central Medical Stores overhaul, phased National Health Insurance)
