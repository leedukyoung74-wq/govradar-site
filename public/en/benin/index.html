Benin

베냉 · BJ

Updated 2026-11-14 · as of 2026-10-08 · Watch

President Wadagni's government approved a 2027 draft budget of CFAF 4,757bn (about $8.1bn, +14.7%) on 7 October. It targets 7.5% growth and a deficit of at most 2.8% of GDP, with five priorities: modern agriculture, industry, tourism, technology and human capital. It also adopted an action programme for 2026-2033.
