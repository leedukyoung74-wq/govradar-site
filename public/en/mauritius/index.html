Key national agenda

  • Cut the fiscal deficit (about 6.0% of GDP to 3.7%)
  • Use AI and digitisation, including public-sector digital transformation
  • Grow start-ups and SMEs (10-year tax holiday for start-ups)
  • Blue economy and expanded economic space
  • Financial services reform (FSC digitalisation, stronger AML/CFT, open banking and tokenisation)
  • Stable water, power and other basic infrastructure
