Key national agenda

  • Government action programme 2024-2028: jobs and purchasing power, defence and security, land planning and infrastructure, basic social services, governance and public finance, environment
  • Responding to the eastern conflict and building peace (ICGLR and regional diplomacy)
  • Raising domestic revenue and improving public finance management
  • Opening the economy through eastern corridors such as Dar es Salaam
  • Expanding roads, power and drinking water
