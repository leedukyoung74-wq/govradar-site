Government · leader

Key national agenda

  • Deficit reduction (5% of GDP target for 2027, €54bn of consolidation)
  • Response to energy and fuel prices (release of strategic diesel stocks)
  • Purchasing power and tax adjustments
  • Youth and education measures for high-school and university students
  • AI and industrial sovereignty through the France 2030 investment plan
