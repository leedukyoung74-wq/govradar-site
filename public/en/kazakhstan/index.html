Government · leader

President Kassym-Jomart Tokayev (head of state), Prime Minister Olzhas Bektenov (head of government, reconfirmed by the Kurultai on 31 Aug 2026), Vice President Yerlan Karin

Key national agenda

  • Implementing political and administrative reform under the new constitution (single-chamber Kurultai, vice presidency)
  • Full adoption of digital and AI tools in government, education and health (2026 Year of Digitalisation and AI)
  • Faster infrastructure and social-facility construction ('the country as one large construction site')
  • Growth of energy, manufacturing (autos, machinery, metals) and logistics, and more foreign investment
  • Stronger rule of law, fiscal discipline and regional development
