Kazakhstan

KZ

Updated 2026-11-14 · as of 2027-20 · Stable

A new constitution adopted by referendum in March 2026 created a single-chamber parliament (Kurultai) and a vice presidency, and Prime Minister Bektenov was reconfirmed. The government declared 2026 the 'Year of Digitalisation and AI' and is stepping up infrastructure building and foreign investment, targeting growth above 5% a year in 2027–2029.
