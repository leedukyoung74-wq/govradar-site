Government · leader

President José Raúl Mulino (head of state and government, term 2024-07 to 2029-07)

Key national agenda

  • Fiscal consolidation and keeping investment grade (Moody's Baa3, S&P BBB-)
  • Canal water security (Río Indio reservoir) and logistics hub expansion
  • Public infrastructure investment in health facilities and roads
  • Financial reputation after removal from the EU tax blacklist
  • Deciding the future of the Cobre Panamá mine (no reopening decision before end-2026)
  • Process toward a new constitution
