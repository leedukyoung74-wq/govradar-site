Country Philippines

The Muslim autonomous region of Mindanao, Philippines. It held its first parliamentary election in September 2026, and the parliament seated on 30 October chose Macacua as the first elected Chief Minister. The 2026 budget of about ₱114bn focuses on education, health and infrastructure; approved investment had already passed the full 2025 total by Q2.

GovernmentParliamentary — 80-seat parliament elects the Chief Minister. Sharia applies to Muslims only

Government · leader

Chief Minister Abdulraof Macacua (elected 30 Oct 2026, 51 votes) · Speaker Amer Rakim

Key national agenda

  • First elected government and an 'inclusive government' (46+ seat coalition)
  • Closing the peace process: decommissioning, assistance packages, camp transformation
  • Delivering the education- and health-led 2026 budget (~₱114bn)
  • Road and farm-to-market networks (JICA road capacity project 2026–2029)
  • Investment promotion in health, tourism and agribusiness
