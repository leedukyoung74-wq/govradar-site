Government · leader

Head of state King Charles III (Governor-General Sir Patrick Allen); Prime Minister Andrew Holness (re-elected September 2025)

Key national agenda

  • Restoring homes, schools and power after Hurricane Melissa (recovery phase to end January 2027)
  • Keeping fiscal stability and raising new, mainly consumption-based revenue
  • Energy transition, including procurement of 300 MW renewables and 150 MW batteries
  • Higher investment in health and education
  • Stronger public security (larger national security budget)
