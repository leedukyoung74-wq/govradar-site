Seven steps to a contract — what you can do and how we help

For each step: what your company does itself, the free official channels to use, and what GOV RADAR and WMM do for you or with you.

Find

What you do

Choose three target countries and one field, register on the multilateral procurement portals and switch on notice alerts.

Decide if you can win

What you do

Check each eligibility requirement (similar experience, turnover, staff, equipment) against your records, and look up past winners and prices in the same field.

How GOV RADAR and WMM help

A tender fit review (₩100,000 per notice, VAT excl.) gives a go/no-go, the gaps and how to close them. The award analysis below shows competitors.

Order a fit review →See awards →

Documents and proposal

What you do

Prepare the bid security, experience certificates, three years of financial statements and certificates in English, and write the technical proposal in the order of the evaluation criteria.

How GOV RADAR and WMM help

Through a project partnership we review the proposal structure and the English version with you (by quote).

Partnership consultation →

Local partner and consortium

What you do

For works and large projects, consider a joint venture or subcontract with a locally registered firm, and sign a letter of intent and an NDA first.

How GOV RADAR and WMM help

CONNECT and consortium matching find local partner candidates; letter-of-intent and NDA templates are ready to use.

Consortium →Contract templates →

Finance and guarantees

What you do

For bid and performance bonds, check K-SURE export guarantee insurance; for market-entry costs, the Public Procurement Service's overseas procurement support programme. Also check overlap with Korean aid projects (EDCF, KOICA).

K-SURE ↗Public Procurement Service (PPS) ↗EDCF ↗KOICA ↗

How GOV RADAR and WMM help

Each country radar has a 'Korean support' section with Korean programmes available there.

Country radars →

After the award

What you do

Check contract terms (payment, delay damages, guarantee periods) and plan the local entity, customs, certification and staffing.

How GOV RADAR and WMM help

A project partnership manages the delivery plan and local cooperation with you.

90-day plan — to your first bid

The order in which a company new to international procurement can submit its first bid or expression of interest within 90 days.

Weeks 1–2

Register and get alerts

Register on UNGM, World Bank and ADB; save three countries; turn on alerts

Weeks 3–4

Pick targets

Pick five pre-tender projects and three open tenders in your field; look up winners in that field

Weeks 5–8

English company file and partners

English track record, certificates and financials; contact three local partner candidates; letter of intent and NDA

Weeks 9–12

First submission

Choose one with a fit review → submit an EOI or bid → record the result and the winning price
