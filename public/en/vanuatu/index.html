Vanuatu

VU · Commonwealth

Updated 2026-11-14 · as of 2026-02 · Stable

In February 2026 Prime Minister Jotham Napat said 'business as usual is no longer safe' and set economic transformation, centred on the economy, health and education, as the core goal. National Sustainable Development Plan targets will be made concrete by 2030, the 50th anniversary of independence. VT2 billion in climate funds is earmarked for resilient roads on Malekula (works expected from 2028), and the World Bank is providing about VT3 billion to strengthen the fisheries ministry.
