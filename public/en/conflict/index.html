US–Israel vs Iran / Strait of Hormuz WAR

War ongoing · transit restricted · US blockade of Iranian ports continues

  • ConfirmedThe war began with US–Israeli strikes on Iran on 28 Feb 2026; Iran has controlled transit through the strait since.[1][2]
  • ConfirmedThe US maintains a naval blockade of Iranian ports (83 vessels redirected as of late Aug, CENTCOM).[3][2]
  • Official statementOn 29 Oct the Panama-flagged tanker Louise 1 was hit by limpet mines in the strait; engine-room fire, no injuries.[4]
  • Party claimIran's IRGC said several tankers hit mines in mid-October; the US denied part of this, saying a drone struck one instead.[2][5]
  • Single sourceIt is reported that the US military is presenting post-midterm (3 Nov) strike options on Iran to the President.[4]
  • ConfirmedPresident Trump ruled out nuclear use and ground troops and said he expects a deal after the midterms.[2][4]
  • Single sourceBrent traded around $117/bbl on 30 Oct, a fourth straight monthly rise.[4]

Review Gulf projects, shipping, insurance and fuel-linked contracts. Screen anything touching Iran for sanctions.
