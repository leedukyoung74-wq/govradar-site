Saudi Arabia (Iran war, Houthi attacks) WAR

Target of Iranian and Houthi missiles and drones · leads coalition air strikes in Yemen

  • ConfirmedAfter the Iran war began on 28 Feb 2026, Iranian missiles and drones hit Saudi refineries; Aramco shut the Ras Tanura refinery.[35][36]
  • ConfirmedRight after the April US–Iran ceasefire, an attack on an East–West pipeline pumping station cut flows by 700,000 bpd (Saudi official). Iran denied involvement.[37][38]
  • ConfirmedIn May Reuters and AP, citing US officials, reported that Saudi Arabia and the UAE secretly struck drone and missile launch sites inside Iran.[39][40]
  • ConfirmedIn early October the Houthis hit southern airports such as Abha and Jizan with missiles and drones, wounding three (Saudi statement); on 8 Oct the coalition said it intercepted three ballistic missiles.[41][42][43]
  • Official statementThe Saudi-led coalition said 100 jets gave air support to the Yemeni government's 'Yemen Dawn' offensive launched on 4 Oct and destroyed 324 Houthi targets.[44]

Re-plan safety for sites and trips in the Eastern Province (refining) and the south (Jizan, Abha, Najran); review Red Sea and Gulf shipping insurance and delivery dates.

US–Israel vs Iran / Strait of Hormuz WAR

War ongoing · transit restricted · US blockade of Iranian ports continues

  • ConfirmedThe war began with US–Israeli strikes on Iran on 28 Feb 2026; Iran has controlled transit through the strait since.[1][2]
  • ConfirmedThe US maintains a naval blockade of Iranian ports (83 vessels redirected as of late Aug, CENTCOM).[3][2]
  • Official statementOn 29 Oct the Panama-flagged tanker Louise 1 was hit by limpet mines in the strait; engine-room fire, no injuries.[4]
  • Party claimIran's IRGC said several tankers hit mines in mid-October; the US denied part of this, saying a drone struck one instead.[2][5]
  • Single sourceIt is reported that the US military is presenting post-midterm (3 Nov) strike options on Iran to the President.[4]
  • ConfirmedPresident Trump ruled out nuclear use and ground troops and said he expects a deal after the midterms.[2][4]
  • Single sourceBrent traded around $117/bbl on 30 Oct, a fourth straight monthly rise.[4]

Review Gulf projects, shipping, insurance and fuel-linked contracts. Screen anything touching Iran for sanctions.

Russia–Ukraine WAR

War ongoing · front-line freeze proposals under discussion

  • ConfirmedOn 21 Oct President Zelensky said he could consider freezing current lines for an immediate ceasefire.[6][7]
  • Single sourceThe Kremlin rejected Ukrainian/European proposals but said trilateral talks with the US and Ukraine could resume around 10 Nov.[6]
  • Single sourcePer ISW, Ukraine has retaken about 1,080 km² since January 2026.[6]
  • ConfirmedRussia says it is open to limited local ceasefires, while attacks continue.[8][9]

Reconstruction (energy, transport, housing) timing depends on talks. Watch EBRD/World Bank reconstruction calls.

Yemen civil war / Bab al-Mandab Strait WAR

Houthi offensive (Sept) and government counter-offensive (Oct) · fight over the strait

  • ConfirmedThe Houthis launched an offensive on 3 Sept, took Mokha, the Red Sea coast and Perim Island, and declared a blockade on Saudi-linked shipping.[45][44]
  • Party claimGovernment forces claimed on 5 Oct to have retaken Bab al-Mandab and Mokha; the Houthis denied it.[46][47]
  • Official statementWHO reported 5,000+ casualties since 6 Aug (959 deaths), about 200,000 displaced and a surge in suspected cholera.[44]

Price in detours and war-risk premiums for cargo via the Red Sea and Suez.

Sudan civil war (army vs RSF) WAR

Army holds Khartoum, RSF holds Darfur · Kordofan is the main front

  • ConfirmedThe army holds Khartoum and the north, east and centre; the RSF holds almost all of Darfur. The army lost El Fasher in Oct 2025.[53][34]
  • Single sourceIOM warns that El Obeid, North Kordofan's capital sheltering about 500,000 people, faces the threat of a full-scale assault.[53]
  • Single sourceUN figures put the internally displaced at about 8.6 million (peak 11.6 million in Jan 2025).[53]

Humanitarian procurement (food, water, health) continues for Sudan, Chad and South Sudan; hold field projects.

Ethiopia (Tigray war resumes, Eritrea involved) WAR

Rebel coalition offensive from 23 Sept · federal army takes Mekelle · Eritrean troops enter

  • ConfirmedOn 23 Sept a rebel coalition including the TPLF launched an offensive in Tigray, Afar and Amhara; the federal army took the Tigray capital Mekelle in early October.[54][55]
  • ConfirmedEthiopia told the UN Security Council that Eritrea's entry into Tigray was an 'all-out invasion'; Eritrea denied it and cut diplomatic ties.[54][56]

Pause work near northern Ethiopia and the Eritrean border; allow for delays in Addis Ababa projects.

Pakistan–Afghanistan war WAR

Cross-border air and artillery strikes since February · flared again in September

  • ConfirmedIt began with Pakistani air strikes in eastern Afghanistan in February and grew into what the defence minister called 'open war'; two holiday ceasefires did not end it.[57][58]
  • ConfirmedFighting flared again after a mass-casualty attack in Kohat in September and lasted more than two weeks.[59][57]

Check movement limits in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan; allow slack in Central Asia overland logistics.

Eastern DR Congo (M23) WAR

JNIM fuel blockade of Mali · 21,000+ deaths in 2026 (region-wide tally)

Hold field work in Mali, Burkina Faso and Niger; review risks on routes via Senegal and Côte d'Ivoire.
