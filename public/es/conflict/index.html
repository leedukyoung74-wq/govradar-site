Saudi Arabia (Iran war, Houthi attacks) GUERRA

Target of Iranian and Houthi missiles and drones · leads coalition air strikes in Yemen

  • ConfirmadoAfter the Iran war began on 28 Feb 2026, Iranian missiles and drones hit Saudi refineries; Aramco shut the Ras Tanura refinery.[35][36]
  • ConfirmadoRight after the April US–Iran ceasefire, an attack on an East–West pipeline pumping station cut flows by 700,000 bpd (Saudi official). Iran denied involvement.[37][38]
  • ConfirmadoIn May Reuters and AP, citing US officials, reported that Saudi Arabia and the UAE secretly struck drone and missile launch sites inside Iran.[39][40]
  • ConfirmadoIn early October the Houthis hit southern airports such as Abha and Jizan with missiles and drones, wounding three (Saudi statement); on 8 Oct the coalition said it intercepted three ballistic missiles.[41][42][43]
  • Declaración oficialThe Saudi-led coalition said 100 jets gave air support to the Yemeni government's 'Yemen Dawn' offensive launched on 4 Oct and destroyed 324 Houthi targets.[44]

Re-plan safety for sites and trips in the Eastern Province (refining) and the south (Jizan, Abha, Najran); review Red Sea and Gulf shipping insurance and delivery dates.

EE. UU.–Israel vs. Irán / Estrecho de Ormuz GUERRA

Guerra en curso · tránsito restringido · continúa el bloqueo de EE. UU. a los puertos iraníes

  • ConfirmadoLa guerra comenzó con los ataques de EE. UU. e Israel contra Irán el 28 de feb. de 2026; desde entonces Irán controla el tránsito por el estrecho.[1][2]
  • ConfirmadoEE. UU. mantiene un bloqueo naval de los puertos iraníes (83 buques desviados a finales de agosto, según CENTCOM).[3][2]
  • Declaración oficialEl 29 de oct. el petrolero de bandera panameña Louise 1 fue alcanzado por minas lapa en el estrecho; incendio en la sala de máquinas, sin heridos.[4]
  • Afirmación de una parteLa IRGC de Irán afirmó que varios petroleros chocaron con minas a mediados de octubre; EE. UU. lo desmintió en parte y dijo que, en cambio, un dron alcanzó a uno.[2][5]
  • Una sola fuenteSe informa de que el ejército de EE. UU. está presentando al Presidente opciones de ataque contra Irán posteriores a las elecciones de mitad de mandato (3 nov).[4]
  • ConfirmadoEl presidente Trump descartó el uso de armas nucleares y de tropas terrestres y dijo que espera un acuerdo después de las elecciones de medio mandato.[2][4]
  • Una sola fuenteEl Brent cotizó en torno a $117/bbl el 30 de octubre, su cuarta subida mensual consecutiva.[4]

Revise proyectos en el Golfo, transporte marítimo, seguros y contratos vinculados al combustible. Verifique sanciones en todo lo relacionado con Irán.

Rusia–Ucrania GUERRA

Guerra en curso · se discuten propuestas de congelar la línea del frente

  • ConfirmadoEl 21 de oct. el presidente Zelensky dijo que podría considerar congelar las líneas actuales para un alto el fuego inmediato.[6][7]
  • Una sola fuenteEl Kremlin rechazó las propuestas ucranianas/europeas, pero dijo que las conversaciones trilaterales con EE. UU. y Ucrania podrían reanudarse hacia el 10 de nov.[6]
  • Una sola fuenteSegún el ISW, Ucrania ha recuperado unos 1,080 km² desde enero de 2026.[6]
  • ConfirmadoRusia dice estar abierta a altos el fuego locales limitados, mientras continúan los ataques.[8][9]

El momento de la reconstrucción (energía, transporte, vivienda) depende de las negociaciones. Siga las convocatorias de reconstrucción del BERD/Banco Mundial.

Yemen civil war / Bab al-Mandab Strait GUERRA

Houthi offensive (Sept) and government counter-offensive (Oct) · fight over the strait

  • ConfirmadoThe Houthis launched an offensive on 3 Sept, took Mokha, the Red Sea coast and Perim Island, and declared a blockade on Saudi-linked shipping.[45][44]
  • Afirmación de una parteGovernment forces claimed on 5 Oct to have retaken Bab al-Mandab and Mokha; the Houthis denied it.[46][47]
  • Declaración oficialWHO reported 5,000+ casualties since 6 Aug (959 deaths), about 200,000 displaced and a surge in suspected cholera.[44]

Price in detours and war-risk premiums for cargo via the Red Sea and Suez.

Gaza Strip and West Bank TENSIÓN

Ceasefire of 10 Oct 2025 in place but strikes continue · rising settler violence in the West Bank

Sudan civil war (army vs RSF) GUERRA

Army holds Khartoum, RSF holds Darfur · Kordofan is the main front

  • ConfirmadoThe army holds Khartoum and the north, east and centre; the RSF holds almost all of Darfur. The army lost El Fasher in Oct 2025.[53][34]
  • Una sola fuenteIOM warns that El Obeid, North Kordofan's capital sheltering about 500,000 people, faces the threat of a full-scale assault.[53]
  • Una sola fuenteUN figures put the internally displaced at about 8.6 million (peak 11.6 million in Jan 2025).[53]

Humanitarian procurement (food, water, health) continues for Sudan, Chad and South Sudan; hold field projects.

Ethiopia (Tigray war resumes, Eritrea involved) GUERRA

Rebel coalition offensive from 23 Sept · federal army takes Mekelle · Eritrean troops enter

  • ConfirmadoOn 23 Sept a rebel coalition including the TPLF launched an offensive in Tigray, Afar and Amhara; the federal army took the Tigray capital Mekelle in early October.[54][55]
  • ConfirmadoEthiopia told the UN Security Council that Eritrea's entry into Tigray was an 'all-out invasion'; Eritrea denied it and cut diplomatic ties.[54][56]

Pause work near northern Ethiopia and the Eritrean border; allow for delays in Addis Ababa projects.

Pakistan–Afghanistan war GUERRA

Cross-border air and artillery strikes since February · flared again in September

  • ConfirmadoIt began with Pakistani air strikes in eastern Afghanistan in February and grew into what the defence minister called 'open war'; two holiday ceasefires did not end it.[57][58]
  • ConfirmadoFighting flared again after a mass-casualty attack in Kohat in September and lasted more than two weeks.[59][57]

Check movement limits in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa and Balochistan; allow slack in Central Asia overland logistics.

Myanmar civil war GUERRA

Junta offensives and resistance fighting continue · 9,600+ deaths in 2026 (tally)

  • Una sola fuenteRecorded combat deaths in 2026 stand at 9,678.[34]
  • Afirmación de una parteState media said in November that the army seized a key resistance headquarters and 10 bases.[60]

Screen sanctions and safety first; allow for delays on Thai and Bangladeshi border logistics.

Eastern DR Congo (M23) GUERRA

Fighting continues despite the Washington accord · about 4,000 deaths in 2026 (tally)

  • ConfirmadoM23 entered Uvira in Dec 2025 right after the US-brokered peace deal and withdrew in Jan 2026, when government troops returned.[61][62]
  • Una sola fuenteRecorded 2026 deaths across the Congolese conflicts stand at 3,991.[34]

Tighten origin due diligence on minerals (cobalt, copper, tin).

Sahel jihadist insurgency (Mali, Burkina Faso, Niger) GUERRA

JNIM fuel blockade of Mali · 21,000+ deaths in 2026 (region-wide tally)

  • ConfirmadoAl-Qaeda-linked JNIM has blockaded fuel to southern Malian cities since Sept 2025 and declared a full blockade of Bamako on 28 Apr 2026.[63][64]
  • Una sola fuenteRecorded 2026 deaths in the Maghreb–Sahel insurgencies stand at 21,049, among the highest in the world.[34]

Hold field work in Mali, Burkina Faso and Niger; review risks on routes via Senegal and Côte d'Ivoire.

Venezuela (US intervention) TENSIÓN

Maduro captured in a US raid in January · Rodríguez acting president

  • ConfirmadoOn 3 Jan the US struck Caracas and captured President Maduro and his wife, flying them to New York; about 80 people died.[65][66]
  • ConfirmadoVice-president Delcy Rodríguez was sworn in as acting president on 5 Jan; US strikes on alleged drug boats in the Caribbean (Operation Southern Spear) continue.[66][67]

Check US sanctions changes weekly before any Venezuela deal.

Tensión · continúan las pruebas de misiles balísticos de la RPDC

  • ConfirmadoJCS: la RPDC lanzó 1 misil balístico desde Wonsan el 3 de octubre y 2 desde Pyongyang el 12 de octubre (uno voló más de 900 km).[10][11]

  • Falso“Varios países están señalando conjuntamente la misma guerra”
