How to sell to the Government of India

تم التحقق في 2026-10-31

Foreign firms can take part, but Make in India local-content preference is strong. Firms from land-border countries (e.g. China) need prior registration.

بوابات المشتريات

  • GeM (Government e-Marketplace)
  • CPPP (Central Public Procurement Portal)

كيفية المشاركة

  1. Track GeM and CPPP (GeM is effectively mandatory for central purchases)
  2. Check your supplier class by local content: Class-I ≥50%, Class-II 20–50%, non-local <20%
  3. Class-I suppliers within the purchase-preference margin (generally 20%) can win over cheaper non-local bids — consider local production or partners
  4. Land-border-country firms must register under GFR Rule 144(xi) (MEA and MHA clearance)
  5. Nodal ministries may set higher local-content thresholds

أفضلية المحلي والقيود

  • Public Procurement (Make in India) Order 2017, amended 2020
  • GFR 2017 Rule 144(xi), effective 23 July 2020
